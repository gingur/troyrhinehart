## Rigor bar for our report

To beat this sheet blind, the report must at minimum reproduce all 79 metrics
(both strategy and benchmark columns where applicable), both tables, and the
14 plot types — or provide a strictly richer superset (e.g., regime breakdowns,
turnover/costs, confidence intervals) while losing none of the above.
