# Final critic verdict for plinko (must be closed)

## Biggest gap
Plinko's simulator surface is off-contract and unchunked. `simulate_provably_fair` feeds n_rounds straight into `bulk.plinko_directions`, materializing the whole (n, rows) float64 matrix: measured peak RSS 1,320 MB at 5M drops and 2,541 MB at 10M drops (rows=16), vs 218 MB for the sibling `Wheel.simulate(10M)` — 5x over the project's stated <500 MB budget, and scripts/validate_plinko.py trips it twice per run. Meanwhile the headline `simulate()` is a numpy-PCG64 binomial surrogate that never touches floor(float*2), so validate's advertised 270M rounds are 270M surrogate rounds with only 30M on the real HMAC stream. Fix: collapse both into the suite's standard `simulate(n_rounds, bulk=None, chunk_rounds=2_000_000, progress=True)` that loops `bulk.plinko_directions(rows, step)` in chunks and accumulates an np.bincount pocket histogram (the Keno/Mines/Wheel pattern), and align the rest of the contract — class `Plinko` not `PlinkoEngine`, `play_round` not `play`, result keys `n_rounds`/`analytic_std_per_unit`/`se_rtp`/`z_score`/`within_3se`. That drops 10M-round peak memory to ~200 MB, puts the headline rounds on the game's own RNG, and removes every tell in the blind module panel — the only panel that still identifies ours as the imitation. (Secondary, unfixed since round 1: `simulate` docstring still says "int8 draws" when gen.binomial returns int64; `play` still accepts server_seed="" and negative nonces.)
